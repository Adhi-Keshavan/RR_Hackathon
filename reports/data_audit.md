## Data quality and interpretation

- `extras1_runs` is constant within each extras category (1=Overstep, 2=wide, 3=legbyes, 4=byes, 5=other, 6=no extras, 7=Penalty). It is a code, not an extras run count. `extras_2_runs` is zero on almost every row, including every wide, bye, and leg-bye. Exact team totals and required run rates cannot be reconstructed safely from these fields yet.
- `over_ball_num` reaches 13; it is a delivery index within an over, not necessarily a legal-ball number. Wides and `Overstep` are treated as illegal. The legality of `other` and `Penalty` is unresolved.
- Revised-overs fields contain values such as 114 and 42, apparently ball counts despite their names. This interpretation needs validation before pressure scoring uses an innings limit.
- 40 playing-XI rows repeat `(match_id, team_id, player_id)`; 35 groups have conflicting batting orders. The normalized roster leaves those orders missing and preserves a conflict flag.
- The playing XI does not cover a small number of delivery roles ({'striker': {'rows_absent_from_playing_eleven': 83, 'rows_absent_from_competition_mapping': 0}, 'non_striker': {'rows_absent_from_playing_eleven': 98, 'rows_absent_from_competition_mapping': 0}, 'bowler': {'rows_absent_from_playing_eleven': 30, 'rows_absent_from_competition_mapping': 0}}). The competition-player mapping does cover them.
- Six matches contain innings 3/4 and require separate super-over handling. Sixteen match metadata rows have no delivery records and cannot contribute to delivery-based ratings.
- The statement describes IPL and T20I data, but the pack also contains two non-IPL T20 competitions (30 matches). The core ranking cohort should use IPL and T20I unless the organizers clarify their inclusion.
- The statement mentions supporting player data, but the archive has no separate `players.csv`; player IDs and names are available in the competition-team-player mapping and delivery records.
- `delivery_ist_time` contains the literal `UTC` in its values. Use `match_date` for season chronology until timezone semantics are confirmed.

Full machine-readable counts and category distributions are in `reports/audit.json`.
